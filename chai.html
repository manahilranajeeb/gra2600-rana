Chai

Spiced, milky tea served with a blend of aromatic spices.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups (480 ml) water
  • 2 cups (480 ml) milk
  • 1 tsp (5 ml) ground cinnamon
  • 2 tsp (10 ml) black tea leaves
  • 2 tsp (10 ml) sugar
  • 4 green cardamom pods, crushed

Method

  1. Combine water and milk in a saucepan.
  2. Add cinnamon and cardamom pods.
  3. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 5 minutes.
  4. Strain the tea into cups and serve with milk if desired.
