Measure for Measure

Measure for Measure is an informational recipe catalog built for cooks who bounce between measurement systems whether that is a U.S. cook working from a European cookbook or an international visitor using an American recipe.

Every recipe on the site lists ingredients in both units side by side, be it cups, ounces, Fahrenheit or grams, milliliters, Celsius. Nothing gets lost in conversion. The catalog is small and tested, covering sauces, baked dishes, desserts, and drinks.
