About This Site

Why Dual Measurements Matter

A cup of flour and 120 grams of flour are the same amount, but only if the recipe tells you so. Recipes from the U.S. use cups and Fahrenheit, while most of the world cooks by weight, milliliters, and Celsius. Listing both units on every line means any cook can follow any recipe without stopping to search for a converter.

My Cooking Background

I cook at home, mostly the dishes I grew up eating and the ones I wanted to learn, like om ali and chai. Moving between recipes written in different units is what inspired this site.

How Conversions Were Verified

Volume amounts use the standard 1 cup = 240 ml and 1 tsp = 5 ml. Dry ingredients differ by density, so each weight was checked against published ingredient charts rather than converted by volume alone. Sources used:

  • King Arthur Baking ingredient weight chart
  • USDA FoodData Central
  • NIST guidance on metric unit conversions
  • Oven temperatures rounded to the nearest 5°C
