Om Ali

A warm and comforting Egyptian dessert made with layers of puff pastry, milk, sugar, and nuts.

  • Prep: 15 minutes
  • Bake: 35 minutes at 400°F (200°C), then 350°F (175°C)
  • Serves: 6-8

Ingredients

  • 1 sheet (250 g) puff pastry
  • 2 cups (480 ml) milk
  • 2 cups (480 ml) water
  • 1 cup (200 g) sugar
  • 1 cup (120 g) ground nuts

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Tear the pastry into pieces and bake in a greased dish for 15 minutes, until golden.
  2. Combine the milk, sugar, and water in a saucepan.
  3. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Layer the baked pastry pieces with the milk mixture and ground nuts in a greased baking dish.
  5. Lower the oven to 350°F (175°C) and bake for another 20 minutes or until the top is golden.
