Om Ali

A warm and comforting Egyptian dessert made with layers of puff pastry, milk, sugar, and nuts.

Ingredients

  • 1 sheet (250g) puff pastry
  • 2 cups (480 ml) milk
  • 1 cup (200 g) sugar
  • 1 cup (200 g) ground nuts
  • 2 cups (480 ml) water

Method

  1. Bake the pastry pieces in a greased baking dish.
  2. Combine the milk, sugar, and water in a saucepan.
  3. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Layer the baked pastry pieces with the milk mixture and ground nuts in a greased baking dish.
