Pizza Sauce

A simple and delicious pizza sauce made with tomatoes, garlic, and herbs.

  • Prep time: 5 minutes
  • Cook time: 15 minutes
  • Makes: about 2 cups (480 ml)

Ingredients

  • 2 cups (480 ml) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp (1 g) dried oregano
  • 1 tsp (5 g) salt

Method

  1. Combine all ingredients in a saucepan.
  2. Simmer over medium-low heat for 10-15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  3. Adjust seasoning to taste.
  4. Use immediately or let cool before storing in the fridge.
