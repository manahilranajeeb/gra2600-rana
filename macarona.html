Macarona Bechamel

Baked pasta with a creamy bechamel sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb (450 g) pasta
  • 1 cup (200 g) butter
  • 1 cup (200 g) all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups (480 ml) milk

Method

  1. Cook the pasta according to package instructions.
  2. In a separate saucepan, melt the butter and whisk in the flour.
  3. Gradually add the milk and stir until smooth.
  4. Simmer for 10 minutes or until thickened.
  5. Serve the bechamel sauce over the cooked pasta.
