Macarona Bechamel

Baked pasta with a creamy bechamel sauce.

  • Prep: 20 minutes
  • Bake: 25 minutes at 375°F (190°C)
  • Serves: 4-6

Ingredients

Pasta

  • 1 lb (450 g) pasta

Minced Meat

  • 1 tbsp (15 ml) vegetable oil
  • 1 medium onion, diced
  • 1 lb (450 g) minced beef
  • 1 tsp (5 g) salt
  • 1/2 tsp (1 g) black pepper

Pizza Sauce

This is the same sauce as our Pizza Sauce recipe.

Béchamel Sauce

  • 1/4 cup (55 g) butter
  • 1/4 cup (30 g) all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups (480 ml) milk
  • 1/2 tsp (3 g) salt

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the pasta according to package instructions, drain, and set aside.
  3. In a large skillet, heat the oil over medium heat. Add the onion and cook until translucent.
  4. Add the minced beef, salt and pepper. Cook for 8-10 minutes or until browned.
  5. Add the pizza sauce to the meat and simmer for 5 minutes.
  6. In a separate saucepan, melt the butter and whisk in the flour.
  7. Gradually add the milk and stir until smooth.
  8. Simmer for 10 minutes or until thickened, then stir in the salt.
  9. Spread half the pasta in a baking dish, cover with the meat sauce, then add the rest of the pasta.
  10. Pour the béchamel sauce over the top and bake for 25 minutes or until golden brown.
